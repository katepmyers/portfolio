A little about me

Hey, I’m Kate.

I’m a journalist from Ruston, Louisiana, studying communication at Mississippi State University. I’m drawn to stories that help people understand the communities and the people around them. I am especially passionate about higher education, data and breaking news reporting.

As editor-in-chief of The Reflector and director of Take30 News, I get to work with student journalists across several different writing, visuals and broadcast mediums. I’m especially excited about finding creative ways to make information accessible, innovative and engaging to a new generation of consumers.
